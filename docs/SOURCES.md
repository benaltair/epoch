# Historical source decisions

The dataset uses authoritative texts and official Bahá’í sources. Each published record carries its own citations; this note records decisions that are easy to confuse.

| Question                                            | Decision and evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bahá’u’lláh’s mission and the thousand-year minimum | October 1852, at month precision, following [Kitáb-i-Aqdas note 62](https://reference.bahai.org/en/t/b/KA/ka-79.html). No endpoint is predicted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The Báb’s ministry versus the Bábí Dispensation     | The earthly ministry ends with the martyrdom on 9 July 1850. The dispensation continues to the intimation of Bahá’u’lláh’s mission in 1852. See the [life of the Báb](https://www.bahai.org/the-bab/life-the-bab) and the Dispensation glossary entry in [Messages 1963–1986](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/muhj-1963-1986/muhj-1963-1986.pdf). |
| Why not use 1853 for every boundary?                | [God Passes By](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/shoghi-effendi/god-passes-by/2) labels historical periods 1844–1853 and 1853–1892. Book periodization is not interchangeable with a ministry’s earthly span or the Aqdas minimum-duration anchor.                                                                                                                                |
| Formative Age epochs                                | The [5 February 1986 statement](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/messages/19860205_001/1) distinguishes the first epoch’s 1944–1946 transition and the separate Divine Plan sequence. Preserve the transition as uncertainty.                                                                                                                      |
| Fifth/sixth Formative Age boundary                  | Year 2022, supported by the title and milestone chronology of [The Fifth Epoch, 2001–2022](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/muhj-2001-2022/muhj-2001-2022.pdf). This is distinct from the third Divine Plan epoch, announced at Riḍván 2021. Year precision avoids treating the announcement month as a universally precise boundary.              |
| Divine Plan epochs                                  | First 1937–1963, second from 1964, third from 2021. The [1986 statement](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/messages/19860205_001/1) and [Riḍván 2021 message](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/messages/20210420_001/1) support their independent sequence.                                         |
| Golden Age                                          | A future, undated age. The 1986 statement establishes its place in the sequence, not calendar boundaries. It stays searchable and readable without an axis position.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Cycle scale                                         | At least 500,000 years, following the [Riḍván 1992 message](https://www.bahai.org/library/authoritative-texts/the-universal-house-of-justice/messages/19920421_001/1). The viewport’s edge is not the cycle’s end.                                                                                                                                                                                 |

Ages, ministries, dispensations and plans are related classifications; their dates need not form one strict containment tree. The Heroic Age is linked to both dispensations. Parent relationships provide navigational context and are reviewed separately from dates. Sources and editorial notes should be rechecked when a future correction changes that relationship.

Plan dates are mostly retained at year precision. An exact Riḍván day should only be added with supporting calendar evidence. The Gregorian viewing range is a display choice, not a declaration about the beginning or end of history. The dataset is an initial collection, not an exhaustive account.
